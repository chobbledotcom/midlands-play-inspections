      Two new sections went up today.

      The first is Guides, which collects the longer explanations that do not
      belong on a service page. It opens with a single entry under Tags and
      paperwork, called Do tested bouncy castles need a tag? The short version
      is that the law asks for an annual inspection by a competent person
      rather than for a tag, and that the tag is what lets a venue check the
      inspection without ringing you first. If you have ever been asked for a
      tag by a school and wondered what they were actually checking, that page
      is the answer.

      The second is case studies, which holds the write-ups that outgrow a blog
      post. There is one up already, from a fleet day, and more will follow as
      jobs happen and as the people we test for are happy to be named.

      If there is a question you have been meaning to ask about inspection
      paperwork, send it over. It might end up as the next guide.

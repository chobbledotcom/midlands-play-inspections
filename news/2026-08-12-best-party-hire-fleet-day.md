      Nine units in a day, which is about as many as you can do properly before
      the light goes. Four standard castles, a bounce-and-slide, a disco dome,
      two toddler units and the soft play, all laid out in the barn ready to go
      before we got there, which is the only reason we got through them all.

      The disco dome took the longest, as they usually do. All that netting
      means a lot of mesh to go over and a lot of seams round the roof panels,
      and the electrical side has to be checked as well as the fabric.

      One of the toddler castles came up with a worn anchor patch on the back
      left corner. It was still inside tolerance, but only just, and it will
      have gone by next summer. Better to know about it in August than find out
      about it in the middle of the season.

      Everything else went through first time of asking. The tags went on before
      we left and the reports went out the same week.

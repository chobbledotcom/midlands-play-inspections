      Not a hire company this time. A primary school near Rugby that bought its
      own soft play set and ball pool a couple of years back and had never had
      either of them looked at, on the reasonable-sounding grounds that they
      weren't hiring anything out.

      That's the bit people get wrong. The duty follows the equipment being used,
      not whether money changed hands for it. If children are on it, someone has
      to be able to show it was checked by a competent person, and a school is
      exactly the sort of place that gets asked.

      Both were in decent condition. A couple of the soft play shapes had lost
      some of their firmness and one of the ball pool wall panels had a seam
      starting to open, which we flagged. Report went over the same week and
      into their health and safety file, which is where the value of it is.

      If you're a school, a nursery or a village hall with equipment sitting in
      a cupboard, it's worth an ask. It's a smaller job than you'd think.

      A hire company just outside Coventry, with a 60ft double-lane obstacle
      course as the centrepiece of the fleet. These are the units that take the
      time. A standard castle you can walk round in three or four minutes once
      it's up. A course that length has climbs, slides, pop-ups, tunnels and
      thirty-odd metres of netting on each side, and every bit of it needs
      looking at.

      The unit itself was in good order. Two of the internal net panels had gone
      brittle at the top edge where they get the most sun, which is what usually
      goes first on a course this age. Neither was through, and both are a
      straightforward repair for someone with a machine.

      Worth saying as well, we couldn't get the van within forty yards of where
      the course had to inflate, so everything got carried. If you've got a long
      unit and somewhere better to lay it out, say so when you book. It's the
      difference between getting your last two units done and coming back
      another day for them.

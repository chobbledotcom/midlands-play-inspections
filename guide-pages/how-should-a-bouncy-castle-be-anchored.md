      Almost every serious inflatable incident comes back to anchorage or wind.
      A castle that isn't held down properly can lift, roll or blow over with
      children on it, and no amount of good stitching or correct air pressure
      makes up for that.


      The short answer is this: use every anchor point the unit has, which will
      be at least six. On grass, use proper metal stakes. On hard ground, use
      ballast or tested ground bolts. And never use the inflatable in winds
      above 24mph.
  - type: html

    content: "## How many anchor points does a bouncy castle need?   HSE's guidance
      says every inflatable needs at least six anchor points. The exact number
      for your unit is set by the manufacturer and should be in its operating
      manual. BS EN 14960-1:2019 includes a method for working out how many a
      unit needs based on its size, so bigger units will usually have more than
      six.   Outdoors, every anchor point must be used, every time. If one is
      missing, torn or has come away from the unit, the castle isn't ready to go
      out."

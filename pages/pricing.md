  - type: faqs

      ## Common questions about cost
    items:
      - question: Do you charge for travel?
        answer: |
          Travel is normally free within Warwickshire and the West Midlands. Beyond
          that it's quoted up front as a single figure, never a per-mile total you
          find out about afterwards. On fleet days, travel is a single charge for
          the day regardless of how many units we get through.
      - question: What if a unit fails?
        answer: |
          You still pay the inspection fee, because the work has been done and you get
          the report either way. The re-check after a repair is usually free if it's
          done while we're still in the area or bundled into your next visit.
      - question: Is there a minimum charge?
        answer: |
          For a single small unit a long way from base, travel can outweigh the
          inspection fee. It's usually worth waiting until you've got two or three
          to do, or joining onto another operator's fleet day nearby. We'll tell
          you if that's the cheaper option.
      - question: Can I pay per unit on a fleet day?
        answer: |
          Yes, if you'd rather. For small fleets it can work out cheaper. We'll
          price it both ways and you take whichever is lower.

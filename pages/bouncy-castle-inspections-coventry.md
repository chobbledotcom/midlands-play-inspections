      ## Who books us in Coventry

      Hire fleets first, then schools, community centres and the odd
      soft play centre. We also get asked to look at units bought secondhand from
      other operators, which is worth doing before the first booking rather than
      after.

      Whatever you've got, the inspection is the same: over 140 check points on
      a standard castle, worked through against BS EN 14960-1:2019, with the tag
      fitted before we leave and the report emailed within a few days. Units
      outside the PIPA scheme get the equivalent inspection and the same report,
      just without a tag.

      Travel to Coventry is free. Fleet days in the city are the cheapest way to get a lot of units through.
